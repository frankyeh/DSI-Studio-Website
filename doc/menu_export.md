# [Export] Menu

Use the **[Export]** menu in the Fiber Tracking window to save per-voxel diffusion metrics, 4D fiber orientation vectors, half-sphere orientation distribution functions (ODFs), attached anatomical or custom slices, and template-warped MNI maps from the open `.fz` or `.fib.gz` session, or to apply in-memory bias-field correction. Rather than listing fixed commands, **[Export]** builds its items dynamically from the loaded FIB file and any currently attached slices, refreshing automatically whenever volumes are added or removed under **[Slices]**.

## Menu items

Items appear in up to three separator-divided groups depending on the contents and coordinate space of the loaded FIB file:

| Menu item | Behavior |
|---|---|
| **`<metric>...`** | Appears once for each available 3D metric or slice in the session (for example, **qa...**, **iso...**, **dti_fa...**, **md...**, **ad...**, **rd...**, **rdi...**, **qir...**, **vol...**, or an attached anatomical slice). Opens a save dialog defaulting to `<fib_stem>_<metric>.nii.gz` with filters for NIfTI (`*nii.gz *.nii`), MATLAB (`.mat`), or all files, and writes the 3D volume on the FIB voxel grid (`dim`), voxel spacing (`vs` in mm), and NIfTI affine header (`trans_to_mni`). Selecting `.mat` writes the raw 3D matrix without NIfTI header transforms (except **color...**, which always writes 3D RGB NIfTI). When exporting an external custom slice whose matrix dimensions differ from the FIB diffusion grid, DSI Studio resamples the volume onto the FIB grid via cubic interpolation using its active slice-to-diffusion registration before saving. |
| **fiber...** | Always listed immediately after the session's 3D metrics. Opens a save dialog defaulting to `<fib_stem>_fiber.nii.gz` and exports all resolved per-voxel unit fiber orientation vectors in FIB voxel space as a 4D floating-point NIfTI volume of shape `(X, Y, Z, 3 × num_fiber)`, ordered sequentially by fiber population (`0` through `num_fiber - 1`) and `(x, y, z)` vector components within each population. Always writes NIfTI with the FIB voxel spacing and header affine. |
| **odfs...** | Listed immediately after **fiber...** only when ODF matrices (`odf0`, …) were stored in the FIB file during Step T2 reconstruction. Opens a save dialog defaulting to `<fib_stem>_odfs.nii.gz` and exports half-sphere ODF values across all voxels in FIB voxel space as a 4D floating-point NIfTI volume of shape `(X, Y, Z, half_odf_size)` (such as `321` directions on the standard 8-fold tessellated sphere). Always writes NIfTI. |
| **`<metric> in mni...`** | Listed below a separator for every session metric (exact lowercase `in mni...` label, such as **qa in mni...**, **iso in mni...**, or **dti_fa in mni...**) **only** when the loaded FIB file is in native subject space rather than template/MNI space (omitted automatically for QSDR and template FIBs). Opens a save dialog defaulting to `<fib_stem>_<metric>.nii.gz`, loads a cached `<fib_file>.<template>.mz` nonlinear mapping or runs dual-modality (`qa` + `iso`, with `rd` fallback) template normalization (`R² >= 0.3` quality gate), resamples the scalar map into the active template space, and writes a 3D NIfTI volume with the template's voxel spacing and MNI affine. (**fiber...** and **odfs...** are never listed in this group because directional vectors and ODFs are not warped or reoriented here.) |
| **Correct Bias Field...** | Listed below a final separator **only** when the session includes an `iso` map (the menu label ends with `...` even though no file dialog opens). Runs immediately with a progress bar, estimates a smooth 3D multiplicative log-bias field from `iso` inside the brain mask while accounting for voxel spacing, and multiplies `iso`, every resolved fiber anisotropy array (`qa` or `fa` across all fiber populations), and any stored `rdi` or `nrdi*` maps in memory by `exp(-log_bias_field)`. |

## Dynamic metric entries

Which **`<metric>...`** (and **`<metric> in mni...`**) items appear depends on the reconstruction method, optional outputs saved during Step T2 reconstruction, whether a template mapping file exists, and which anatomical or custom slices are loaded in the session:

| Menu item | Behavior |
|---|---|
| **qa...** / **fa...** | Exports the primary resolved fiber anisotropy volume (`fa0`) as a 3D floating-point map: quantitative anisotropy (**qa...**) on multi-fiber GQI/QSDR reconstructions, or single-fiber fractional anisotropy (**fa...**) on single-fiber DTI and 2D histology FIB files. |
| **qir...** | Exports the isotropy-normalized quantitative anisotropy ratio as a 3D scalar volume. Automatically synthesized on multi-fiber, non-database FIB files that contain `iso` by normalizing `qa` against outlier-limited `iso`. |
| **iso...** | Exports the isotropic diffusion component from GQI or QSDR reconstruction as a 3D scalar volume. Presence of `iso` also enables **Correct Bias Field...** at the bottom of the menu. |
| **dti_fa...**, **md...**, **ad...**, **rd...**, **rd1...**, **rd2...** | Exports diffusion tensor fractional anisotropy (**dti_fa...**) and mean, axial, radial, second-eigenvalue, or third-eigenvalue diffusivities (in `10⁻³ mm²/s`) as 3D scalar volumes whenever those tensor maps were saved in the FIB file during reconstruction. |
| **gfa...**, **rdi...**, **nrdi02L...** (and `nrdi*L...`), **ha...**, **txx...**–**tzz...**, **hraw...** | Exports optional scalar reconstruction maps when stored in the FIB file: generalized fractional anisotropy (**gfa...**), restricted diffusion imaging (**rdi...**), non-restricted diffusion imaging at specific diffusion sampling lengths (**nrdi02L...**, **nrdi04L...**, …), tensor helix angle in degrees (**ha...**), symmetric diffusion tensor components (**txx...**, **txy...**, **txz...**, **tyy...**, **tyz...**, **tzz...**), or raw histology intensity (**hraw...**). |
| **vol...** | Exports the 3D relative volume (Jacobian determinant) map quantifying local tissue expansion or contraction relative to the template. Stored directly in QSDR FIBs; on native-space FIBs, **vol...** appears in the menu once a `<fib_file>.<template>.mz` normalization mapping file exists on disk (or after `vol` has been initialized in the slice selector dropdown), with brain-mask mean Jacobian normalized to `1.0` and zero-anisotropy background voxels set to `1.0`. |
| **color...** | Exports a stored directional color map as a 24-bit RGB 3D NIfTI volume across the FIB voxel grid (`.mat` output is not used for **color...**). |
| **t1w_template...**, **t2w_template...**, **wm_template...**, or **`<slice_name>...`** | Exports reference template volumes (automatically attached for QSDR/MNI FIBs), companion `.nii.gz` images auto-loaded beside BIDS `sub-*` FIB files, modalities embedded during reconstruction, or external 3D volumes added via **Slices › Insert Other Images...** and **Slices › Insert MNI Images...**. Exporting from **Export** writes the volume on the FIB diffusion grid (`dim`, `vs`), whereas **Slices › Save Slices as...** saves a custom slice on its own native voxel grid. |

## Coordinate, registration, and bias-field cautions

- **Rename default filenames when saving MNI exports:** Both native **`<metric>...`** and template-warped **`<metric> in mni...`** pre-fill the save dialog with `<fib_stem>_<metric>.nii.gz`. Give MNI exports a distinct suffix such as `.mni.nii.gz` so they do not overwrite native-space exports—and so DSI Studio automatically recognizes them as MNI-space volumes if loaded later as reference maps in [Differential Tractography](/doc/gui_t3_dt.html).
- **Save MNI-warped maps as NIfTI, never `.mat`:** Choosing a `.mat` filename writes the unwarped native-grid slice matrix directly before template warping runs. Always save **`<metric> in mni...`** items as `.nii.gz` or `.nii`.
- **Wait for slice registration and check custom-slice grid behavior:** Before exporting an external volume inserted via **[Slices][Insert Other Images...]**, wait until background rigid-body registration completes. Note two grid behaviors for inserted custom slices: first, **[Export]** resamples custom slices onto the FIB diffusion grid (`dim`) only when their matrix dimensions (`X × Y × Z`) differ from `dim`, so an inserted volume that already has the exact same matrix dimensions as the FIB file is written without rigid-body resampling; second, **`<metric> in mni...`** indexes the slice volume directly with the FIB-to-template deformation rather than chaining a separate custom-slice registration, so export non-diffusion-grid custom slices to FIB space first or use **[Slices][Save Slices as...]** to save a custom slice in its own native voxel space (preserving skull-stripping or burned-in tracts and regions).
- **Understand the in-memory scope of bias-field correction:** Clicking **[Export][Correct Bias Field...]** updates `iso`, resolved fiber anisotropy (`qa` / `fa`), and stored `rdi` / `nrdi*` maps in memory for the open window only. It does not overwrite the `.fz` / `.fib.gz` file on disk unless you subsequently save a copy with **[File][Save FIB As...]**, does not recompute DTI tensor maps (`dti_fa`, `md`, `ad`, `rd`) or already-cached `qir` values, and does not set a one-time completion guard—clicking **[Export][Correct Bias Field...]** multiple times in the same session re-estimates and applies additional multiplicative passes.

## Related exports in other menus

Because **[Export]** is dedicated to FIB-grid voxel volumes and template-warped metric maps, other Fiber Tracking file, table, and scene exports live in their respective object menus:

- **Modified FIB files and workspaces:** Use **[File][Save FIB As...]** to write the current session (including in-memory bias-field correction) to a `.fz` file, or **[File][Save Workspace...]** to bundle tracts, regions, devices, slices, and camera settings—see the [File menu](/doc/menu_file.html).
- **Tractography bundles, track-density images (TDI), connectivity matrices, and tract statistics:** Use **[Tracts][Save Current Tract As...]** or **[Tracts][Save All Tracts As ...]** (`.tt.gz`, `.trk.gz`, `.tck`, `.mat`, `.obj`), **[Tracts][Save Tracts (Misc)...][Save Tract Density Images...]** / **[Tracts][Save Tracts (Misc)...][Save Tract Density Images(X2)...]**, **[Tracts][Save Tracts (Misc)...][Save Endpoints As...]**, **[Tracts][Save Along Tract Index...]**, **[Tracts][Tract Profile...]**, **[Tracts][Connectivity matrix...]**, **[Tracts][Statistics...]**, or **[Tracts][Tract-Atlas Intersection Statistics...]**—see the [Tracts menu](/doc/menu_tracts.html).
- **Regions of interest, region statistics, and voxel tables:** Use **[Regions][Save Current Region As...]**, **[Regions][Save Checked Regions to one 3D NIFTI...]**, **[Regions][Save Checked Regions to multiple 3D NIFTIs...]**, **[Regions][Save Checked Regions to one 4D NIFTI...]**, **[Regions][Statistics...]**, **[Regions][Region-Atlas Intersection Statistics...]**, or **[Regions][Tract-To-Region Connectome...]** ([Regions menu](/doc/menu_regions.html)), and **[Regions Misc][Save Voxel Data As...]** ([Regions Misc menu](/doc/menu_regions_misc.html)).
- **Custom slice volumes, DICOM series, and slice registrations:** Use **[Slices][Save Slices as...]** to save an inserted slice on its own native voxel grid (including skull-stripping or burned-in tracts and regions), **[Slices][Save Slices to DICOM...]**, or **[Slices][Save Slice Registration...]**—see the [Slices menu](/doc/menu_slices.html).
- **Stereotactic devices, screenshots, videos, and 3D meshes:** Use **[Devices][Save Device As...]**, **[Devices][Save All Devices As...]**, or **[Devices][Statistics...]** ([Devices menu](/doc/menu_devices.html)) for `.dv.csv` trajectories and contact coordinates, and **[Screen][Save 3D Screen...]**, **[Screen][Record Video...]**, or **[Screen][Save 3D Model...]** (`.obj` / `.glb`) ([Screen menu](/doc/menu_screen.html)) for publication figures and 3D surface models. For batch metric exports across multiple subjects, see [Export Data](/doc/cli_exp.html).
